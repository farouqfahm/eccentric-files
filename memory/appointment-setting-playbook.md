# Appointment Setting Playbook

*Source: YouTube video transcript (2026-02-05)*

## The Core Concept

Start an **appointment setting service business** — you book qualified appointments for B2B companies, they pay you **$100-$200 per appointment** that shows up.

**Why it works:**
- You only need to learn 2 skills: setting appointments + sales calls
- No service delivery to learn (your appointment setting skill IS the delivery)
- Creates a flywheel: better at getting your own clients → better at getting clients for clients → repeat

## The Model

1. **Pick a B2B niche** — businesses that serve other businesses (marketing agencies, business consultants, corporate financial advisers, recruiters, etc.)

2. **Match contact method to niche** — the method you use to reach YOUR clients should be the same method you'll use to reach THEIR clients:
   - **Cold calling** → if phone numbers are easy to find
   - **Cold email** → if owner emails are easy to find
   - **Cold DM** → if social accounts are easy to find (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok)

3. **The pitch to clients:**
   > "I'll set qualified, ready-to-buy appointments for you. You pay me per appointment that shows up. $150 per call. I can probably get you 1-2 a day. You pay me once the appointment is booked."

4. **Get paid weekly** — invoice clients, charge their card on file

## Examples

| Your Niche | Contact Method | Their Niche | Contact Method |
|------------|----------------|-------------|----------------|
| Marketing agencies | Cold call | Gyms | Cold call |
| Business consultants | Cold call | Agricultural businesses | Cold call |
| Online fitness coaches | Instagram DM | Professionals/mums | LinkedIn/IG DM |

## The Math

- $100-$200 per qualified appointment
- 1-2 appointments per day = full-time income
- 4-5 hours of work per day
- 7 appointments/week @ $150 = $1,050/week = **$4,200/month**

## Key Points

- B2B only (businesses serving businesses)
- Charge $100-$200 minimum per call — anything less means the niche isn't viable
- Learn the skills free on YouTube (cold calling, cold DM, cold email, high-ticket sales)
- Your acquisition skill becomes your delivery skill (flywheel effect)
- "Boring and monotonous but it's financial freedom"

## The Script Framework

**For booking appointments (calling prospects for your client):**
> "Hey, I work for [consultant/agency] who helps [target niche] do [benefit 1], [benefit 2], and [benefit 3]. I'd love to put you in touch with him. Are you available on [day] at [time]?"

**For getting your own clients:**
> "Hello, I want to set appointments for you — qualified, ready-to-buy appointments. I want you to pay me per appointment. $150 per call that's qualified and shows up. I can probably get you 1-2 a day. You pay me once the appointment is booked. How does that sound?"
